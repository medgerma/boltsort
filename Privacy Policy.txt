Privacy Policy
This policy explains how the mobile game BoltSort ("the App"), developed by Screenshots, handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect
We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any account. The App does not require sign-in.

Your game progress (current level, coins, and sound setting) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect:

Your device's advertising ID
IP address and approximate location derived from it
Device information (model, operating system version, language)
Interactions with ads (views and clicks) and diagnostic data
This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

3. Data sharing
We do not sell or share any personal information. The only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, as described above.

4. Data security
Data sent to Google AdMob is encrypted in transit.

5. Children
The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact
If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: medgerma903@gmail.com
